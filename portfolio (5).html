My Approach

How I work on every project

01
Understand the Problem

Before writing a single line of code, I dig into the actual problem. For Smart Canteen, that meant walking through the cafeteria, observing pain points, and mapping the entire order flow from student to counter staff.

Discovery
02
Plan the Architecture

I design the database schema, user flows, and page structure before touching code. A solid plan means fewer rewrites and cleaner output.

Architecture
03
Build & Iterate

I develop in focused sprints — front-end first, then integrating the back-end, then wiring the database. I test each layer before moving on.

Development
04
Test & Refine

I test with real scenarios, edge cases, and different devices. Clean, bug-free delivery is non-negotiable — whether it's for a professor or a professional client.

Quality
05
Deliver & Improve

Ship it, then keep improving. I believe no project is truly "done" — there's always a smarter query, a cleaner function, or a better UX decision waiting to be made.
